!!! info "Two model generations on this page — the deployment one leads"

    The **deployment generation** is what the scorecard recommends and what every
    dollar figure on this site is summed from: **Gemini 3.6 Flash with the
    reasoning budget minimised** (`gemini-flash-current-capped`). Its figures come
    first in every table below.

    The **development generation** — Gemini 2.5 Flash — is where the adaptation
    ladder and the prompt-optimizer work were done. Those prompts were then run
    **unchanged** on the deployment generation and the instruction rules kept
    working. That portability is a finding in its own right, and it is why both
    generations stay on this page: deleting the older one would delete the
    evidence that the rules transfer.

    Every model, its provider ID, its region and the window its calls were
    recorded in: [Models in this study](../models-in-this-study.md).
